Overview

Welcome to Raffle Bowl (also known as Palabunutan), developed by vincemavill. This Privacy Policy clarifies how the mobile application operates regarding user data and privacy.

We strongly believe privacy is a fundamental right. Raffle Bowl is designed from the ground up as a standalone, offline-friendly utility for randomized draws, games, raffles, and traditional Filipino "bunutan" paper picking without requiring user tracking or online surveillance.

2

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal identifiable information (PII).

When you download and use Raffle Bowl, we do not request or gather:

  • Your name, username, or contact information (email, phone, address).
  • Your geographical location (GPS or network IP-based coordinates).
  • Your device identifiers, advertising IDs, or hardware serial numbers.
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or camera feeds.
  • Payment or credit card information.
Key Guarantee: No registration, sign-in, or account creation is required to use any feature of Raffle Bowl.
3

Local Data Storage (On-Device Only)

Raffle Bowl saves the items you enter (such as participant names, raffle tickets, bowl titles, presets, and winner draw history) exclusively within your device’s local sandbox storage (using React Native AsyncStorage).

  • No Cloud Syncing: Your raffle entries and history are never sent over the internet or uploaded to any cloud server.
  • Full Ownership: You retain complete ownership and sovereignty over your raffle data.
  • Isolated Storage: Other applications on your device cannot access Raffle Bowl's private data sandbox.
4

Device Sensors & Permissions

To deliver an engaging, realistic simulation of drawing paper tickets from a raffle bowl, the app makes use of specific device hardware capabilities:

  • Motion Sensors (Accelerometer): Used locally in real-time to detect physical shaking of your phone. Shaking the device shakes the raffle bowl and tumbles the paper tickets using the in-app physics engine. Accelerometer readings are processed immediately in memory and are never recorded, logged, or transmitted anywhere.
  • Haptic Feedback / Vibration: Provides tactile feedback when shaking the bowl or revealing a drawn lot.

Raffle Bowl does not request background location, camera, microphone, or address book permissions.

5

Third-Party Services and Analytics

Many modern apps bundle third-party tracking, advertising, and marketing SDKs. Raffle Bowl does not.

  • No advertising networks (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network).
  • No tracking or user profiling services.
  • No external telemetry frameworks.
6

Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Raffle Bowl is a safe, family-friendly utility suitable for general audiences of all ages, including classroom raffles, family games, and children's party activities.

Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, Raffle Bowl complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and applicable international privacy frameworks. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions).

7

Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data lives strictly on your local device:

  • Manual Reset: You can delete any individual raffle bowl or clear your draw history at any time from within the app interface.
  • Clear App Storage: Clearing app storage in your device Settings completely removes all saved data.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the app permanently purges all saved bowls and preferences from your device.
